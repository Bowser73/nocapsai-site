Twizted Journeys Case Study

NoCapsAI helped Twizted Journeys organize a nonprofit digital system that included website updates, donation pathways, event pages, QR campaigns, merch and fundraiser sections, resource links, admin workflow support, and grant opportunity tracking.

The problem

Twizted Journeys had a real mission, real community work, and growing outreach needs, but the digital system needed to be easier to understand, easier to update, and easier to connect to flyers, events, donations, and future funding opportunities.

The NoCapsAI build

  • Updated website structure and messaging
  • Donation and fundraiser pathways
  • Event pages and outreach pages
  • QR code campaign destinations
  • Merch and fundraiser sections
  • Community resource links
  • Admin workflow support
  • Grant tracking support

The result

Twizted Journeys now has a stronger online hub that can support events, QR flyers, donations, merch, resources, and future grant-readiness work from one connected system.
